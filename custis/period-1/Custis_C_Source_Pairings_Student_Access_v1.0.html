Student Reading Version

This legal record shows George Washington acting as guardian for John Parke (Jacky) and Martha Parke (Patsy) Custis after Daniel Parke Custis's death. Read it as evidence of legal responsibility over the children and their property. Guardianship did not erase the separate legal identity of Custis property, including property connected to enslaved people.

The Appomattox surrender terms record the agreement under which Robert E. Lee's Army of Northern Virginia surrendered to Ulysses S. Grant on April 9, 1865.
